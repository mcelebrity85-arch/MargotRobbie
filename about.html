About

The woman behind the work.

Margot Elise Robbie is an Australian actress and producer whose career has moved, in barely more than a decade, from a Queensland television set to the centre of international cinema — and, increasingly, to the producer's chair behind it.

Born on the Gold Coast and raised with a directness that still colours her work, she trained in performance before winning a role on Neighbours in 2008. The transition to American film came in 2013, and with it a run of performances that refused to repeat themselves: the sharp edge of The Wolf of Wall Street, the physicality of Suicide Squad, the wounded ambition of I, Tonya — a role that earned her an Academy Award nomination.

Alongside acting, she co-founded the production company LuckyChap, which has become known for championing bold, director-led material and for taking genuine creative risk. It is that combination — star power and quiet authorship — that defines her, and it is the reason a meeting with her is such a rare and considered thing.

“A private audience is not a photograph. It is an hour of someone's attention — and it is treated as such.”
