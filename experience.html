The Experience

How a meeting happens.

Every audience is arranged the same way: quietly, in order, and with the approval of management at each stage. There are four steps, and no shortcuts.

01

Submit an Enquiry

Send a written enquiry through the Contact Management page. Tell us who you are, the nature of the occasion, and the preferred window of time.

02

Review & Verification

Management reviews every enquiry personally. Suitable requests are verified, and availability is checked against the current schedule.

03

Confirmation & Arrangement

If the request is approved, management confirms the date, the format, the location and all terms directly with you — in writing.

04

The Meeting

A private, unhurried audience, conducted with the discretion and courtesy expected on both sides. Photography is by prior agreement only.

Discretion

Enquiries are treated as confidential. Names, dates and details are not disclosed, published or shared with any third party at any stage.

Etiquette

Audiences are courteous and time-bound. Guests are asked to arrive promptly, keep to the agreed duration, and respect the privacy of the artist and her team.

Availability

Only a small number of engagements are accepted each year, subject to filming and production commitments. A submitted enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Important All scheduling, rates and payment arrangements are quoted and handled directly and exclusively by management. This website does not collect payment information of any kind. Any request for payment made outside management correspondence should be treated as invalid.
