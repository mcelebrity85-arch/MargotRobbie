Biography

A career in chapters.

Selected milestones from a body of work that has moved deliberately from performance to production — presented here for the benefit of prospective guests.

1990

Gold Coast, Queensland

Born Margot Elise Robbie, the third of four children. Raised on the coast, she began performing young — by her teens she was working professionally while completing school.

2008 — 2011

Neighbours & the Australian Screen

Cast as Donna Freedman on the long-running series Neighbours, she appeared in more than three hundred episodes — the apprenticeship that established both her discipline and her range.

2013

The American Arrival

The Wolf of Wall Street introduced her to an international audience, followed swiftly by Focus and The Legend of Tarzan. The transition was immediate and permanent.

2014

LuckyChap Entertainment

Co-founded with Tom Ackerley, Josey McNamara and Sophia Kerr — a production home built to develop and champion material that might otherwise go unmade.

2016

Harley Quinn

Suicide Squad introduced a performance that entered popular culture almost instantly, and one she would return to and reinterpret on her own terms.

2017

I, Tonya — Academy Recognition

Her portrayal of Tonya Harding earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Actress, alongside a BAFTA nomination and widespread critical acclaim.

2019 — 2020

Once Upon a Time… & Birds of Prey

A supporting turn in Tarantino's Hollywood fable, a second Oscar nomination cycle for Bombshell, and then Birds of Prey — a Harley Quinn story told with LuckyChap behind it.

2023

Barbie & the Producer's Chair

Barbie — produced through LuckyChap and starring Robbie — became a global cultural event. The film confirmed what the industry had begun to understand: her influence now extends well beyond the frame.

Present

Production, Selectivity & Privacy

Her schedule is deliberately limited. A small number of private engagements are accepted each year, arranged entirely through management and never through third parties.
