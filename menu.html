The Menu

Experiences, à la carte.

A selection of formats available for private arrangement. Each is tailored on confirmation; nothing is standard, and nothing is automatic.

Margot Elise Robbie

Private Engagements · By Appointment

I. Private Audiences

The Quiet Audience On request An unhurried private meeting for a small party. The most limited and most personal format on the menu.
The Evening Reception On request A hosted reception with a curated guest list, arranged in a private venue with full discretion.

II. Industry & Press

The Press Roundtable On request A moderated, time-bound conversation with a limited number of accredited journalists. Questions submitted in advance.
The Studio Session On request A scheduled sitting for an approved publication, broadcast or campaign, with agreed usage terms.

III. Premieres & Screenings

Red Carpet Access On request Attendance at an agreed premiere or official event, coordinated with the event's own press protocol.
Private Screening & Q&A On request A closed screening followed by a short conversation, arranged for a private or institutional audience.

IV. Curated Dinners

The Chef's Table On request An intimate dinner for a small number of guests, seated at a single table, in a private dining room.
The Long Lunch On request A daytime format, lighter in tone and shorter in duration — often the preferred choice for first introductions.

V. Mentorship & Masterclass

The Producer's Masterclass On request A closed session on development, producing and creative risk — offered to a limited number of participants.
One-to-One Mentorship On request A single, focused conversation for an emerging actor or producer. By application, and rarely granted.

Rates, availability and all payment arrangements are quoted and handled directly and exclusively by management.
